About 24 Ethno Avenue

24 Ethno Avenue is a real showroom and brand reinterpreting Ukrainian identity through the language of modern cut. We don’t make costume “ethnic wear” — we make clothing where heritage is felt in the details, not shouted at first glance.

01

Made in Ukraine

Every piece is sewn in Ukraine — from cutting the fabric to final quality control before shipping.

02

Handmade Craft

Embroidery, edge finishing and decorative details are done by hand — so every piece is slightly different.

03

Quiet Luxury

Minimal decoration, maximum attention to cut, fabric and fit — luxury that’s felt, not flaunted.
